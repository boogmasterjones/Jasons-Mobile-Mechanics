  - question: "How do I know if my alternator is failing?"
    answer: "A failing alternator typically shows up as a battery warning light, dimming headlights or interior lights, a dead battery despite a recent replacement, or a whining or grinding noise from the engine bay. Voltage testing confirms whether the alternator is putting out a charge."
  - question: "What are the signs of a bad starter?"
    answer: "A bad starter usually causes a single loud click or rapid clicking when you turn the key, a grinding noise on startup, or a car that cranks very slowly. A no-crank condition with a fully charged battery is the most common sign."
