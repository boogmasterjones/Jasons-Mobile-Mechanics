Mobile Auto Repair Serving Cape Coral, FL

Cape Coral is one of the largest cities in Florida by land area, with over 400 miles of canals winding through its residential neighborhoods — more navigable waterways than any other city in the world. That canal network means the city is enormous and spread out, with many neighborhoods accessed by a limited number of bridges and arterial roads like Pine Island Road, Burnt Store Road, and Del Prado Boulevard. Getting a vehicle towed from a canal-front driveway in the northwest part of the city to a shop on the east side can mean a very long wait and a big bill before any wrenches turn.

Jason's Mobile Mechanics serves Cape Coral by coming directly to your property, across the Midpoint Bridge or Cape Coral Bridge from our Port Charlotte base. We handle brake jobs, battery service, oil changes, diagnostic scans, and pre-purchase inspections at your home or business. Travel time varies depending on which part of the city you're in — call ahead and we'll give you an accurate ETA.

Common Reasons Cape Coral Drivers Call Us

  • Dead battery in a canal-front driveway far from the nearest service shop
  • Brake noise on the long drives up Pine Island Road or Burnt Store Road
  • Check engine light that's been on since the last season
  • Oil changes for snowbirds who've returned to find their vehicle overdue for service
  • Pre-purchase inspections on used vehicles in Cape Coral's active private-sale market
