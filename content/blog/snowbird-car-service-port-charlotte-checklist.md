## Why Cars Struggle After Sitting Through a Florida Summer

A car that sits for months isn't resting. Heat, humidity and time keep working on it. 

- **A weak or dead battery.** Batteries slowly drain even when the car is off, and heat is hard on them.
- **Old oil.** Motor oil ages even when you don't drive. If the last oil change was before you left, it's worth looking at when you return.
- **AC that blows warm.** After a long break, the system may not cool the way it should.
- **Belts and coolant.** Rubber belts can dry out and crack in the heat, and coolant levels or condition can change. Both matter for keeping the engine from overheating.
- **Warning lights.** A check engine light or other dash warning after storage can point to anything from a minor sensor issue to something that needs attention before you drive far.

### 3. Check the AC

Turn the AC on and give it a few minutes. If it's blowing warm, weak, or making unusual noises, it may need a recharge or a repair. AC repair and recharge is one of the services Jason's Mobile Mechanics offers on-site.

### 4. Look at Belts and Coolant

The serpentine belt drives important parts like the alternator and AC compressor. Serpentine belt replacement and coolant flush and radiator service are both available on-site if you need them.

### 5. Don't Ignore a Warning Light

If a check engine light or another warning came on after storage, it's best to find out why before a long drive. Jason's Mobile Mechanics offers engine diagnostics to help find the cause.
