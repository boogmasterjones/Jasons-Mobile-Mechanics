Mobile Auto Repair Services in Port Charlotte, FL

Jason's Mobile Mechanics brings a full range of automotive repair services directly to your location in Port Charlotte and throughout Charlotte County. Instead of arranging a tow, scheduling a drop-off, and waiting for a call from the shop, you stay home or at work while the repair happens where the car is parked. We handle everything from routine oil changes and battery replacements to AC compressor swaps, fuel pump replacements, and suspension repairs — all on-site, without a lift for the majority of jobs.

Southwest Florida puts specific demands on vehicles: the year-round heat degrades rubber components and AC systems faster than cooler climates, salt air from the coast accelerates electrical corrosion and fuel line deterioration, and Florida's ethanol-blended fuel can be harder on injectors and fuel pumps in cars that sit for extended periods. Our service coverage is built around the real failure patterns we see in Charlotte County, Sarasota County, and Lee County — which is why AC repair, cooling system service, and electrical diagnosis are among our most frequently requested calls alongside the standard brakes, batteries, and oil changes.
