Mobile Mechanic Serving Port Charlotte, FL

Port Charlotte is a community of roughly 60,000 residents spread across a wide, canal-laced stretch of Charlotte County between the Peace River and US-41. It's a great place to live — until your car won't start in the Publix lot on Tamiami Trail, or your brakes start grinding on the way to Bayshore Live Oak Park. That's exactly the situation Jason's Mobile Mechanics was built for: a fully equipped mobile repair service that drives to your car instead of asking you to drive to it.

We handle the repairs that make up the bulk of what Port Charlotte drivers actually need — brake jobs, dead or dying batteries, oil and fluid service, check-engine-light diagnostics, and pre-purchase inspections for anyone shopping the used car lots along US-41. Whether you're in the canal-front neighborhoods off Harborview Road, near the Sunseeker Resort corridor, or out toward Kings Highway and Murdock, we come to your driveway, your workplace parking lot, or wherever your vehicle happens to be stuck.
