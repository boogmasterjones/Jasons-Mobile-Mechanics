  - question: "How often should I flush my coolant?"
    answer: "Most manufacturers recommend a coolant flush every 30,000–50,000 miles or every 2–5 years, depending on the coolant type. In Florida's heat, keeping coolant fresh is especially important because degraded coolant loses its ability to prevent corrosion inside the cooling system, which leads to premature water pump and radiator failure."
  - question: "What causes a car to overheat?"
    answer: "Overheating is most commonly caused by low coolant from a leak, a stuck thermostat, a failing water pump, a clogged radiator, or a blown head gasket. A pressure test and visual inspection pinpoint the cause before the engine suffers permanent damage."
  - question: "Can I drive with the temperature gauge in the red?"
    answer: "No. Pull over and shut off the engine as soon as safely possible. Driving even a short distance with an overheating engine can warp the cylinder head, damage head gasket seals, or seize the engine entirely — all far more expensive than the original cooling system repair."
