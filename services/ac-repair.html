Mobile AC Repair & Recharge in Port Charlotte, FL

In Charlotte County, a car AC that stops working isn't a minor inconvenience — it's a safety issue in the summer heat. When your AC starts blowing warm air, making noise, or losing cold gradually over a few weeks, the most common cause is a refrigerant leak slowly draining the system. Jason's Mobile Mechanics carries AC recovery and recharge equipment to your location, runs a pressure test to confirm whether the system holds refrigerant, and performs the recharge or repair on-site without a shop drop-off. Most AC recharges in Port Charlotte take under an hour.

Beyond a simple recharge, we diagnose and repair the full AC system: compressors that are seized or not engaging, condensers that are blocked by road debris, evaporators leaking inside the dash, and blower motors that have stopped moving air. We carry parts for the most common vehicle makes and can handle most repairs in a single visit at your home, office, or apartment complex parking lot. Port Charlotte summers are brutal enough without spending half a day at a shop — we bring the tools to you.

Common AC Problems We Diagnose and Fix

  • AC blowing warm or room-temperature air
  • Cold air only at highway speeds, not at idle
  • AC cycling on and off rapidly
  • Grinding, rattling, or squealing from the compressor area
  • Weak airflow from vents even at maximum fan speed
  • AC cold on driver side, warm on passenger side (blend door issue)
  • Musty or mildew smell from the vents
