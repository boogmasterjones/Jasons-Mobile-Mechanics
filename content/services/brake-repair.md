        <h3>Brake System Components We Service</h3>
        <p>A complete brake job is more than swapping pads on old rotors. Florida's salt air and humidity accelerate corrosion on brake hardware — the slides, pins, and caliper brackets that keep pads moving freely against the rotor. A stuck slide pin causes the inner pad to wear much faster than the outer, producing a brake job that feels uneven and returns in half the normal mileage. Jason's Mobile Mechanics cleans and lubricates all brake hardware with every pad and rotor replacement, and replaces slide pins and hardware kits when corrosion has gone past what cleaning can address. This is the difference between a brake job that lasts two years and one that needs to be redone in eight months.</p>
        <p>Brake fluid is another overlooked component in the Southwest Florida climate. Glycol-based brake fluid is hygroscopic — it absorbs moisture from the air over time — and Florida's humidity accelerates that absorption. Moisture-contaminated fluid has a significantly lower boiling point, which means brake fade under sustained hard use can happen sooner than drivers expect. A brake fluid flush every two years is an inexpensive service that keeps the system performing as designed and extends the life of your calipers and ABS components.</p>
