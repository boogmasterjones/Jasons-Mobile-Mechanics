        <h2>Mobile Spark Plug Replacement &amp; Tune-Up in Port Charlotte, FL</h2>
        <p>Spark plugs are a wear item — they erode slowly over tens of thousands of miles and the symptoms build up gradually, which means many drivers don't notice the decline until the misfire is bad enough to trigger the check engine light. A worn plug causes incomplete combustion: the engine runs rough, fuel economy drops, power feels flat under acceleration, and in severe cases the misfiring cylinder pushes unburned fuel into the catalytic converter and damages it. Jason's Mobile Mechanics replaces spark plugs at your home in Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda, or anywhere in Charlotte County, typically finishing a full set on a four- or six-cylinder engine in under 90 minutes.</p>
        <p>A complete tune-up goes beyond just the plugs. We inspect each ignition coil while the plug boots are off, clean the throttle body if there's carbon buildup causing a rough idle at stops, replace the engine air filter if it's overdue, and run a diagnostic scan to surface any other codes stored in the computer. Everything is done on-site at your location, so the car stays in your driveway instead of spending the day at a shop. Platinum and iridium plugs are available for vehicles that spec them — these last two to three times longer than standard copper plugs and are worth the small price difference for high-mileage daily drivers.</p>
        <h3>Symptoms of Worn Spark Plugs or Ignition Issues</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Rough or shaky idle at red lights or in park</li>
          <li>Engine hesitation or stumbling during acceleration</li>
          <li>Check engine light with a misfire code (P030X)</li>
          <li>Noticeable drop in fuel economy over recent months</li>
          <li>Difficulty starting in the morning, especially in humidity</li>
          <li>Engine surging or hunting at highway cruise speed</li>
          <li>Strong fuel smell from the exhaust</li>
        </ul>
