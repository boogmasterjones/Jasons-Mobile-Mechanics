Pre-Purchase Car Inspection in Port Charlotte, FL

Buying a used car is one of the largest financial decisions most people make, and in Southwest Florida's active private-sale market — where snowbirds sell before heading north and local classifieds move vehicles quickly — there's rarely time for a leisurely shop evaluation. A pre-purchase inspection from Jason's Mobile Mechanics puts an independent mechanic at the seller's location to go over the vehicle before money changes hands.

We check the things that aren't visible in photos or apparent in a short test drive: stored fault codes the seller may have cleared, brake pad thickness, transmission and differential fluid condition, suspension wear, fluid leaks, battery and alternator health, and structural indicators of accident history. You'll leave the inspection with a clear picture of what the vehicle is actually worth versus what the seller is asking — and what repairs, if any, you'd be inheriting.

Why Get an Independent Inspection?

  • A Carfax report shows accident history but doesn't tell you if repairs were done correctly
  • Dealer inspections are done by the same party who benefits from selling you the car
  • Cosmetically clean vehicles routinely have hidden mechanical problems in Florida's humidity and heat
  • A $150–$200 inspection can save you thousands in post-purchase repairs
