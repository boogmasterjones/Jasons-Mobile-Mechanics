Mobile Auto Repair Serving Fort Myers, FL

Fort Myers is the largest city in Lee County and the commercial hub of Southwest Florida, with a dense network of neighborhoods stretching from the revitalized River District along the Caloosahatchee to the sprawling suburban corridors of Colonial Boulevard and College Parkway. Heavy traffic on US-41 and I-75 means plenty of stop-and-go driving that's tough on brakes and cooling systems, and the same Florida heat that bakes every other county in the region accelerates battery degradation and fluid breakdown here too.

Jason's Mobile Mechanics serves Fort Myers and surrounding Lee County communities, performing brake jobs, battery service, oil changes, diagnostics, and pre-purchase inspections at your home, business, or wherever your vehicle is located. We cross the Caloosahatchee bridges to reach Fort Myers from our Port Charlotte base, so travel time varies — call ahead for an accurate arrival estimate on the day you need service.

Common Reasons Fort Myers Drivers Call Us

  • Brake wear after stop-and-go commuting on US-41 or Colonial Boulevard
  • Dead battery in a parking lot near the Edison Ford Winter Estates or downtown River District
  • Check engine light before a longer drive up I-75 toward Tampa or south toward Naples
  • Oil changes for residents who want to skip the chain quick-lube experience
  • Pre-purchase inspections on vehicles from Fort Myers's active used-car market

Nearby Areas We Also Serve

In addition to Fort Myers, Jason's Mobile Mechanics regularly services Cape Coral and the rest of Lee County, as well as Port Charlotte and Charlotte County.
