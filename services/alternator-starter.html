Mobile Alternator & Starter Replacement in Port Charlotte, FL

A failed alternator or a dead starter is one of the most disruptive breakdowns a driver can face — the car starts fine one day and refuses to turn over the next, often at the worst possible time. Jason's Mobile Mechanics handles both components on-site in Port Charlotte and throughout Charlotte County. Before any parts are ordered, we test the alternator output voltage and the starter draw to confirm the diagnosis, so you're not replacing a good alternator when a corroded cable is the real culprit.

Alternator and starter replacement are well-suited to mobile service because the job doesn't require a lift — the vehicle stays exactly where it is while we work. We carry reman alternators and starters for most domestic and import vehicles and can complete the majority of these replacements in a single two-hour visit. A dead battery from a failing alternator is also an easy add-on: we test and replace the battery in the same appointment so the new alternator starts with a healthy charge to maintain.

Alternator, Starter, and Charging System Problems We Fix

  • Battery warning light on the dashboard
  • Dead battery despite being recently replaced
  • Headlights or dash lights dimming at idle
  • Single loud click or no sound at all when turning the key
  • Rapid clicking (relay chattering) on startup attempt
  • Grinding or whirring noise when starting
  • Engine cranks very slowly before starting
